# Project Statement

## Project Title

Smart Student Expense & Budget Analyzer

## Problem Statement

Students may spend money on food, transport, study materials and entertainment without keeping a proper record. This can make it difficult to understand their spending.

The Smart Student Expense & Budget Analyzer is a simple Python program that allows students to record expenses, manage a budget, track income and manage savings goals.

## Objectives

1. To record student expenses.
2. To search and filter expenses.
3. To edit and delete expenses.
4. To set monthly and category budgets.
5. To calculate simple spending statistics.
6. To track income and financial goals.
7. To generate a simple financial report.

## Target Users

College students who want to keep a simple record of their personal expenses.

## Scope

The project is a command-line Python application. Data is saved locally in JSON files.

## Main Modules

- Expense Management
- Budget Management
- Analytics
- Financial Goals
- Income Management
- Report Generation
